# Mentorship & Professional Development

My commitment to knowledge as a common good extends to shaping the next generation of library professionals. As a sessional instructor in LIS programs at [HTWK Leipzig](https://www.htwk-leipzig.de/en/studieren/studiengaenge/masterstudiengaenge/bibliotheks-und-informationswissenschaft-master) and [HföD Munich](https://www.aiv.hfoed.de/en/homepage.html), I teach emerging topics — AI literacy, prompt engineering, citizen science, digital preservation — to future librarians and public administrators.

I currently co-supervise a Master's thesis at HTWK Leipzig on the impact of LLM-based AI on library search, and I co-chair a [Community of Practice on AI application](https://www.uvic.ca/library/) at UVic Libraries, where I support colleagues in navigating emerging technologies in their daily practice.

I see teaching and mentorship as inseparable from my research: the questions my students and colleagues ask shape the problems I work on.
