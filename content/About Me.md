## Where I Come From

I grew up in the 1980s in the German Democratic Republic ('East Germany'), a satellite state of the Soviet Union, where knowledge was a tool of the ruling class. Textbooks, songs, children's literature, school curricula — all shaped to control what we understood about the world. The political class decided for us what was worth knowing.

## Who Shaped Me

<div class="float-section"><div class="float-right" style="max-width: 220px;"><img src="/Pasted image 20260304205815.png" alt="My grandfather was a bricklayer (ca. 1963)" /><span class="caption">My grandfather (ca. 1963)—farmer, bricklayer and storyteller. Photo: private collection.</span></div><p>
My family is blue-collar: farmers, bricklayers, and builders. Hard workers, but also avid readers and storytellers who pass on their experiences and knowledge across generations.</p><p>On my father's side are the readers. Like my grandmother, he always kept shelves filled with books — unusual for a farming family — with narratives different from those we heard at school. They both knew their collections were a family treasure, but one that multiplied through sharing, and so they generously lent their books to friends, neighbors, and community members.</p><p>My mother's side carries the storytellers. She and my grandfather shared a gift for using anecdotes, fables, and tales to keep memories alive and to make sense of the world around us. Their stories were full of humor and wit, but also tragedy and loss.</p><p>These private libraries and kitchen-table stories were my first encounters with knowledge that challenged dominant narratives. They taught me that access to information is always a political question.</p></div>

## How I Got Here

After the Wall came down, I became the first in my family to attend university. I then trained as a professional bookseller — a centuries-old trade in Germany. In 2015, I graduated from HTWK Leipzig with a librarian degree.</p><p>Along the way I came to understand that both the independent book trade and libraries are built on the conviction that knowledge should be accessible to everyone.</p></div>
